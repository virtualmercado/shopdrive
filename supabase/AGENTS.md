Why: counting and sending cannot diverge, and draft persistence must not permit incomplete processing.
- Campaign images are resolved by `_campaign_master_image` from global media_files plus the existing media-library object, with canonical public URL and a frozen media reference; media triggers and restrictive storage mutation policies retain referenced assets. Why: arbitrary or tenant URLs, deletion and overwrite must not corrupt sent emails; preview and confirmation use the same extended renderer.

- Editorial drafts are owner-readable and writable only through revision-checked RPCs; private preview files use a Storage-API-provisioned bucket and reservations binding draft/source revisions to server publication baselines. Why: native bucket provisioning and explicit grants preserve private preview/public catalog separation without direct client writes.
- Custom-domain verification is applied only through service-role apply_custom_domain_verification, locking and snapshot-checking config/access/domain before atomic logs and state; exact DNS scope remains in last_verification_result independently of mutable pilot authorization, and transient resolver outages preserve verified scope. Why: revoked or stale observations must not activate domains or implicitly authorize the root.
- `checkout_payment_attempts` is service-role-only (explicitly revoke default public grants); order creation uses row-locked `create_checkout_order_once` over trusted-pricing/reservation RPCs, and charge claim/result/application use server-only RPCs. Why: capability hashes must never be public, external calls are single-flight, and failed local confirmation must replay the recorded result without charging.
- Subscription notifications claim leased, fenced receipts only after HMAC validation, GET authoritative provider resources, and reconcile through service-only `apply_master_subscription_webhook`; invoice targeting is transaction-local in the existing sync function, and separate payment application receipts bind entitlement to invoice plan/cycle. Why: concurrent/retried deliveries must commit invoice, payment and activation together without touching newer plans or overwriting the initial recurring invoice.
- MARCA secondary with own paid plan: link allowed only while the own subscription is `active` with future `current_period_end`; while linked, own paid subs cannot be created/extended/reactivated, the resolver ignores them after period end (falls to inherited MARCA, so billing cron skips them), `apply_confirmed_plan_downgrade` no-ops for unlimited stores, and unlink closes own subs whose period ended. - Selected-store authorization requires owner identity or independently approved active membership under the direct MARCA owner; operational views and tenant-folder Storage policies (INSERT included) bind request headers to that permission via `merchant_request_store_id()`, while profiles, gateway secrets and subscriptions remain actor-owned. Why: tenant management must not delegate account identity or credentials, and skipped INSERT policies broke delegated product images and category icons.

Why: Auth creation and SQL linking cannot share a transaction, so the server must cap two stores and leave no orphan accounts.

Why: no false success/duplicate customer, and editing a customer must never change historical labels.
