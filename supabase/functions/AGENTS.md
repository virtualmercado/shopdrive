- Jobs internos autenticam por `x-internal-job-token` via `verify_internal_job_token` (token em `internal_job_tokens`); relatório MARCA envia só após `claim_brand_network_report_send` e nunca reenvia `delivery_unknown`. Why: SMTP sem idempotência.
- Campaign image display sizing is a pure dimension-based helper used by the shared HTML renderer; replacing that renderer only affects new renders, never saved HTML, media metadata or queue processing. Why: presentation refinements must preserve historical evidence and preview/send parity.
- Subscription notifications claim leased, fenced receipts only after HMAC validation, GET authoritative provider resources, and reconcile through service-only `apply_master_subscription_webhook`; invoice targeting is transaction-local in the existing sync function, and separate payment application receipts bind entitlement to invoice plan/cycle. Why: concurrent/retried deliveries must commit invoice, payment and activation together without touching newer plans or overwriting the initial recurring invoice.
- Recurrence cancellation claims are row-locked and token-fenced; sent/unknown/expired claims permit reconciliation GET only and never another PUT. Why: ambiguous external outcomes cannot safely be retried.
- Merchant AI functions treat `x-shopdrive-store-id` as the selected store only after `can_actor_manage_marca_store`/`can_manage_owned_store`; product references and logs are bound to that store. Why: linked MARCA stores keep their AI entitlement without cross-tenant attribution.
- MARCA second-store creation goes only through `create-marca-second-store`: a lease-fenced `marca_store_creation_requests` claim (service-role RPCs begin/complete/fail, one pending per brand account, idempotency key per actor) wraps Auth user creation, and `complete_*` links slot 2 plus one approved membership atomically; expired claims hand their unlinked user over for removal. Why: Auth creation and SQL linking cannot share a transaction, so the server must cap two stores and leave no orphan accounts.
- Melhor Envio/Correios quotes are normalized only in `supabase/functions/_shared/melhorEnvioQuotes.ts` (shared by `calculate-melhor-envio` and checkout): per-service status, availability only from a real provider price, per-unit insurance_value, masked summary logs. Why: a local Mini Envios veto, double-counted insurance and dropped provider errors produced false unavailability.
- Linked MARCA secondaries have no subscription to downgrade: the billing job calls per-store service-role `apply_lapsed_secondary_plan_limit` (store lock, re-resolve, then `apply_confirmed_plan_downgrade`) when one resolves to a limited plan. Why: own-period end after the primary lapsed fires no trigger, so products stayed unlimited.
- Delinquent-subscription regularization reuses the same subscription: service-only ledger `master_subscription_regularizations` (one pending per subscription), terms from `_subscription_regularization_terms` (amount from the subscription's own price/paid invoice, official price only as fallback; contract is legal evidence only; external recurrence excluded), charge via `regularize-master-subscription` (begin/record/expire RPCs, stable idempotency key); webhook and that function's poll settle only through `apply_master_subscription_webhook`, and the generic status poll skips regularization payments. Why: no new subscription/contract, no parallel activation, unpaid PIX never cancels the contracted plan.
- Asaas BaaS transport and credential cryptography live only in `_shared/asaasClient.ts` and `_shared/asaasCrypto.ts`; Phase 2 permits explicit Sandbox only, `asaas-sandbox-diagnostic` requires admin and performs one fixed read-only GET with no database writes. Why: environment confusion, credential exposure and diagnostic side effects must remain impossible.
